04 · FAQ

Common questions.

From training heads and ISS officers evaluating the loop.

How is this different from an LMS?

An LMS tracks whether a course was completed. KarmSetu diagnoses the specific competency gap, traces it to a missing prerequisite, and only then prescribes practice — then re-checks that the gap actually closed.

What does “Competency Passport” mean?

A living record per officer, updated after every re-assessment cycle, showing verified capability rather than a list of attended sessions.

Can it plug into existing training infrastructure?

Yes. KarmSetu sits alongside existing LMS and HR systems and ingests assessment data through standard integrations, with no disruption to current workflows.

Is officer data secure?

Assessment data is handled under government data-security requirements for India’s Official Statistical System. Nothing is used outside the assessment loop it was collected for.

How are the adaptive MCQs generated?

An AI model generates questions targeted at the exact prerequisite gap identified in the root-cause step — not from a fixed question bank, so every set is unique to the officer’s diagnosed gap.
